Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Who this covers

Clive is a private AI assistant used by one household. The only Google account it connects to is its own (clivemclendon@gmail.com). It is not offered to the public.

What data Clive accesses

When authorized, Clive accesses Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets and Google Contacts on its own account, plus any files or calendars the household chooses to share with it.

How the data is used

Data is used only to carry out tasks the household asks for, such as reading and replying to email, tracking events, and organizing documents. It is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone outside the household.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored encrypted on a private computer owned by the household. Content may be processed by the AI model provider that powers Clive, solely to complete the requested task.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Clive's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
